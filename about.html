About CEKO

We deliver compassionate, person-centered care and management services that enhance dignity, independence and community well-being.

Who we are

CEKO is a special place for senior citizens. Our holistic approach to care focuses on physical, emotional and social needs.

By creating individualized care plans, we help our residents maintain their independence, their dignity and their overall well-being.

  • Personalized care, tailored to each resident
  • A holistic approach to body, mind and community
  • Promoting independence in daily life

Our charitable mission

CEKO is a foundation as well as a care home. Beyond our residents, we work to empower the less privileged through support, education and community outreach.

Empowering hope, transforming lives: together we build hope.
