What residents and families can count on

Eight commitments shape the care, the home and the support we give to families.

  • Access to quality care

    Trained staff on hand day and night.

  • Social benefits

    Company, conversation and a way to get out.

  • Safety and security

    A secure home with help close by.

  • Affordability

    Fees that take income into account.

  • Cultural sensitivity

    Care that respects where each person comes from.

  • Health and wellness

    Health services, good food and regular exercise.

  • Family support

    Help for the people who care about our residents.

  • Community integration

    Residents stay part of the wider community.

Who we are

CEKO is a special place for senior citizens. Our holistic approach to care focuses on physical, emotional and social needs.

By creating individualized care plans, we help our residents maintain their independence, their dignity and their overall well-being.
