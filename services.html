What we offer

Quality care, a safe home and real support for families, at a cost that takes each person’s circumstances into account.

Access to quality care

  • Trained staff providing 24/7 care and support
  • Medical assistance and monitoring
  • Assistance with daily living activities (ADLs)

Social benefits

  • Opportunities for socialization and community engagement
  • Reduced feelings of loneliness and isolation
  • Transportation to local events and appointments

Safety and security

  • Secure and monitored living environment
  • Emergency response systems
  • Protection from abuse and neglect

Affordability

  • Sliding scale fees based on income
  • Acceptance of government assistance programs
  • Financial assistance and subsidies available

Cultural sensitivity

  • Staff trained in cultural competency
  • Services tailored to meet the unique needs of underserved communities
  • Celebration of diverse cultures and traditions

Health and wellness

  • On-site health services, including therapy and counseling
  • Healthy meal options and nutrition education
  • Opportunities for physical activity and exercise

Family support

  • Respite care for family caregivers
  • Support groups for family members
  • Education and resources for caregivers

Community integration

  • Partnerships with local community organizations
  • Opportunities for residents to participate in community events
  • Transportation to local appointments and services

Above all, a home away from home

Our assisted living facility is a house first: shared meals, familiar faces and rooms that feel lived in. Everything above is delivered inside that.
